Our story

A softer pace,
poured by hand.

Ember began with a simple wish: to make the everyday pause feel as precious as the celebration.

Born in Phnom Penh

Scent, memory, place.

Ember began with a passion for creating handcrafted candles that bring comfort, warmth, and beautiful scents into everyday spaces.

We kept returning to the same feeling: the city cooling after sunset, jasmine carrying through an open window, the hush that arrives once the day lets go. Those small moments became our scent library.

Today, every candle is still made in small batches, finished by hand, and designed to become part of your own memory of home.
